THE VERIFICATION FLOW

One record. Checks that explain the result.

01

Issue

An approved institution signs learner and course details with its own key.

02

Present

The learner shares a credential or QR verification link with an employer.

03

Verify

The employer checks the signature, issuer, expiry, revocation and printed details.

THE IMPORTANT DIFFERENCE

A genuine QR can still be copied.

The demo compares what is printed on a certificate with the signed name and course. A copied QR remains cryptographically genuine, but the document mismatch is shown clearly.
